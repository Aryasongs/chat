Questions people ask.

Is marsX end-to-end encrypted?

Yes. Your chats are end-to-end encrypted, so only you and the person you are talking to can read them.

Is marsX free?

Yes, marsX is free to use for messaging, voice calls and video calls.

Can I use marsX on more than one device?

Yes. Open Settings, choose Link a device and scan the QR code to use marsX on another device.

Can I report or block someone on marsX?

Yes. You can report a contact to our moderators or block them at any time from their contact info.

Do I need a phone number to sign up?

No. You sign up with your email address, then choose a name and a short bio.

Is marsX available on mobile?

Yes. marsX works in any modern browser on Android, iOS, Windows and macOS, and you can install it to your home screen from the browser menu.

Can I make group chats?

Yes. You can create a group, add your contacts and chat together.

How do I add someone on marsX?

Share your marsX ID with them or scan their QR code. Once you connect, you can start a private chat.

Does marsX show ads or track me?

No. marsX has no ads and no tracking.
