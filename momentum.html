Questions

Is the diet plan free?

Yes. Request it with the form above and you get an instant estimate. Our team then emails a full desi meal plan.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. The workouts and diets work at home too. Gym membership plans are coming soon.

Can I follow a vegetarian or vegan plan?

Yes. Choose your food preference in the form and your plan will use dal, paneer, soya, chana, curd and similar foods.

Is this medical advice?

No. It is general guidance. If you have diabetes, a thyroid issue, heart or kidney problems, or are pregnant, speak to your doctor first.

What is Kashish AI?

Kashish AI is our assistant. Tap the button at the bottom right and ask it anything about food, protein, calories or your plan.
